AI-assisted development was used to plan the architecture, scaffold the Vite and Express applications, define validation rules, and generate focused tests.

## Representative prompts

- Design a human-in-the-loop marketplace listing review workflow.
- Separate deterministic validation from AI-generated policy findings.
- Validate all Gemini citations against the policy sections supplied in the prompt.
- Preserve original listings and append-only review decisions.
